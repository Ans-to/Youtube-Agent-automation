## Sources for every number on screen
- **89 % of FX transactions involve USD** — Bank for International Settlements, Triennial Central Bank Survey 2025 (reported 2025/26; 88 % in 2022).
- **≈57 % of allocated global FX reserves in USD; EUR ≈20 %, JPY ≈6 %, GBP ≈5 %, CNY ≈2 %** — IMF COFER (2024: 58 %; Q3 2025 ≈57 %).
- **72 % reserve share in 2001** — IMF COFER historical series (peak ≈72 % in 2001).
- **1944 Bretton Woods, 44 nations, $35 per troy ounce of gold** — Bretton Woods Agreements, July 1944.
- **1971 end of gold convertibility; 1974 oil-in-dollars arrangements ("petrodollar")** — standard monetary history; phrased on screen as the mechanism ("oil is priced in dollars"), not a single treaty claim.
