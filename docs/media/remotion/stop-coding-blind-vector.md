# Stop Coding Blind — SVG detective revision

The isometric voxel workspace, terminal, keyboard and desk are preserved. The former voxel robot has been removed and replaced by a detailed, clean film-noir detective drawn as inline SVG geometry on a camera-facing plane. Its subtle bob and arm gestures follow the 120 BPM beat; the camera, spring-timed words, red emphasis, glitch impacts and CRT scanlines remain.

The current rendered MP4 is published as the `stop-coding-blind-mp4` artifact by the [GitHub Actions Remotion workflow](https://github.com/Ans-to/Youtube-Agent-automation/actions/workflows/render-stop-coding-blind.yml). Download the artifact from the latest successful run. The composition is 1080×1920, 30 fps and 10 seconds (300 frames).

The video remains a draft until it is watched and listened to. Automated typechecking, timing tests and stream verification do not replace human review.
